### Descriptions:
- **Bird**: Both DNA and amino acid alignment sequence for 14972 exon in 363 avian species.
- **Green Plants**: Both the amino acid and DNA alignment for over one thousand Viridiplantae species
- **Sac fungi**: Both DNA and amino acid Alignments of 1107 representatives of the phylum Ascomycota and 6 outgroup species in 815 BUSCO genes
- **Budding Yeast**: Same amino acid alignment dataset used in Qmaker paper to train Q.yeast
- **Cyanobacteria**:  Both DNA and amino acid alignments of a subset of 746 genes from the 773 single-copy ortholog genes in the “cyanobacteria_odb10” database used by BUSCO, which are conserved across all Cyanobacteria.
